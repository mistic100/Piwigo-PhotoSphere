Use this page to configure how your Photo Sphere is displayed as well as creating "virtual tours" by linking Photo Spheres together with clickable markers.

Settings tab

On this this tab you can configure the general look of your panorama. Most importantly the "Orientation correction" section is used to fix the orientation of your camera after the shot. You should use these settings to ensure every panorama is oriented the same (for example with front facing North), so links between panorama are coherent.

Note: the Photo Sphere Viewer library supports embedded XMP metadata which are added my most 360° camera, so you can choose to edit the XMP data yourself before upload (GPano:Pose parameters).

Setting the default orientation when first loading a Photo Sphere is done through the "Center of interest" tab.

Linked photos

This is where you will link multiple panorama together. First click "Add a new link" button to select one of the available Photo Spheres (only images in the same albums as the current image are available) then click on the panorama to place the marker.
You will be asked if you want to also create the returning link from the target panorama, if you choose so it will be placed at a symmetric position, but this can be modified later.

After adding links your can change the marker position by clicking the button and click on the panorama to the new position.

The icon indicates that a returning link exist from the target panorama.

Click on the icon to open the editing page of the corresponding panorama.

Finally use the button to visualize all panoramas linked together. Here yon can click the on an arrow to remove the link (both direction) or click on a thumbnail to open the corresponding editing page.

In the main plugin configuration page you can change how the links are represented and their size.
